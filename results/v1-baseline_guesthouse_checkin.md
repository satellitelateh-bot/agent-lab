# guesthouse_checkin (v1-baseline)

TASK: Sea Breeze Guesthouse in Krabi Town has 8 rooms, check-in 14:00, check-out 11:00, self check-in with key box code sent on arrival day, free Wi-Fi, no breakfast, 5 minutes walk to the night market. Plan the guest messages, then write one check-in instruction message in both Thai and English.

**Guest‑Message Sequence for Sea Breeze Guesthouse (Krabi Town)**  

1. **Booking Confirmation** – Thank guest, confirm reservation of one of the 8 rooms, state dates, check‑in 14:00, check‑out 11:00, self‑check‑in, free Wi‑Fi, no breakfast, night market ≈ 5‑minute walk.  

2. **Pre‑Arrival (1 day before)** – Reminder of arrival date & 14:00 check‑in, re‑state self‑check‑in, note that the key‑box code will be sent on the arrival day morning, include Wi‑Fi network name, and night‑market proximity.  

3. **Arrival‑Day (morning)** – Send key‑box code with clear box location & opening steps, Wi‑Fi password, and brief “welcome” note.  

4. **During Stay (optional mid‑stay)** – Confirm Wi‑Fi is working, remind guest there is no breakfast, and suggest the nearby night market for meals.  

5. **Check‑out Reminder (day of departure)** – Re‑state 11:00 check‑out, ask guest to leave the key box closed, thank them for staying, and invite a review.

Dear Guest,

Welcome to Sea Breeze Guesthouse in Krabi Town. We look forward to hosting you.

**Check-in:** Check-in starts at 14:00.

**Self check-in:** You will check in yourself. On the morning of your arrival day, we will send you the key box code. Use that code to open the key box and collect your room key.

**Wi-Fi:** Free Wi-Fi is available for guests.

**Night market:** The night market is a 5-minute walk from the guesthouse.

Please note that breakfast is not included.

If you have any questions before you arrive, please contact us.

We wish you a pleasant stay.

Warm regards,
Sea Breeze Guesthouse
