# cafe_caption (v2-assumptions)

TASK: Bean There Cafe is a small coffee shop in Phuket Old Town (2 staff, about 80 customers a day, half are tourists, AI budget 1,000 THB a month, posts on Instagram and LINE, open 8:00-17:00, signature drink: coconut cold brew 95 THB). Plan how they start using AI, then write one Instagram caption in English.

**Tool:** Claude or ChatGPT free plan. [Assumed: free tier, 0 THB/month; Owner to confirm current pricing]

**Weekly routine (Mon, Wed, Fri):**

- **Staff A drafts** 2 Instagram captions and 2 LINE messages at 15:00-15:15, during a quiet spell at the counter. [Owner to confirm: peak hours]
- **Staff B reviews** those drafts at 8:00-8:15 the next morning, before the first customers. Check prices (coconut cold brew 95 THB) and hours (8:00-17:00).
- **Instagram:** Staff B schedules approved posts in Meta Business Suite (free). [Assumed: account is linked]
- **LINE:** Staff B schedules approved messages with LINE Official Account Manager's scheduled broadcast feature. [Owner to confirm: feature is available on your account]
- **Swap roles** each week so both staff draft and review.

"Escape the beach chaos and find your island vibe in Phuket's charming Old Town! Stop by Bean There Cafe for our signature Coconut Cold Brew, expertly brewed to refresh you before the sun sets. Visit us before we close at 17:00 and indulge in this creamy delight (95 THB) - your taste buds will thank you! #PhuketOldTown #BeanThereCafe #CoconutColdBrew"

**Monthly AI tracking (Owner or Staff B, 1st of each month, 15 minutes)**

- **Spend:** Log AI tool cost against the 1,000 THB cap. [Assumed: free tier, 0 THB]
- **Engagement:** Record Instagram likes, comments, and saves per post, plus LINE replies per message.
- **Baseline:** Month 1 is the baseline only. No decision until Month 2.
- **Small numbers:** If LINE replies are under 10 in a month [Assumed threshold], report "insufficient data" instead of a percentage change.
- **Decision rules** ("flat" means within ±10% [Assumed threshold]):
  - **Keep:** Engagement or LINE replies up over 10%, spend within cap.
  - **Change:** Mixed results, adjust captions or posting times.
  - **Drop:** Flat or down for 3 months, or spend nears the cap with no gain.
- **Caveat:** Weather, tourist season, and posting times also move these numbers. Treat changes as signals, not proof the AI caused them.
