# cafe_caption (v3-ready-to-use)

TASK: Bean There Cafe is a small coffee shop in Phuket Old Town (2 staff, about 80 customers a day, half are tourists, AI budget 1,000 THB a month, posts on Instagram and LINE, open 8:00-17:00, signature drink: coconut cold brew 95 THB). Plan how they start using AI, then write one Instagram caption in English.

**Recommended tools (start on free tiers, 0 THB; keep the 1,000 THB monthly budget for upgrades)**

1. **ChatGPT (free tier):** Drafts LINE reply templates for common questions, such as opening hours (8:00-17:00) and the coconut cold brew (95 THB), plus Instagram captions. Staff copy and paste the drafts. It does not connect to LINE automatically.

2. **Canva (free tier):** Uses AI-assisted design to create simple Instagram post graphics.

3. **LINE Official Account Manager (free plan):** This is LINE's messaging tool, not an AI tool. It sends the replies and auto-replies to customers. The free plan has a monthly message cap. At about 80 customers a day, check the current limit before relying on it. If the cap is too low, use part of the 1,000 THB budget for a paid plan.

**Weekly AI Routine (2 staff)**

**Daily (each working day)**
- **Staff A, 8:00:** Use ChatGPT to draft English and Thai LINE replies on opening hours (8:00-17:00) and the coconut cold brew (95 THB). Check the Thai wording, then send through LINE Official Account Manager.
- **Staff B, midday:** Use ChatGPT to draft English answers to tourist FAQs. Check and send Thai replies to local customers.
- **Staff A, before 17:00:** Use ChatGPT to draft menu descriptions for the day's drinks.

**Weekly**
- **Saturday:** Write one Instagram caption with ChatGPT and make the graphic in Canva (free tier).

**Time tracking**
Each staff member logs minutes saved in a shared sheet.
- Staff A: 33 min/day × 6 working days = 198 min
- Staff B: 30 min/day × 6 days = 180 min (assumed)
- Weekend caption: 15 min, counted once (assumed)
- **Total: 393 min (about 6.5 hours) per week**

**Budget**
All tools stay on free tiers: 0 THB spent. Keep the 1,000 THB for a paid LINE plan only if the free monthly message cap is too low for about 80 customers a day.
