# cafe_caption (v1-baseline)

TASK: Bean There Cafe is a small coffee shop in Phuket Old Town (2 staff, about 80 customers a day, half are tourists, AI budget 1,000 THB a month, posts on Instagram and LINE, open 8:00-17:00, signature drink: coconut cold brew 95 THB). Plan how they start using AI, then write one Instagram caption in English.

**Workflow audit (2 staff, ~80 customers/day, open 08:00–17:00)**

- **LINE replies:** Staff answer repeated questions on hours, menu, and orders by hand. High volume and repetitive.
- **Instagram posting:** Captions are written from scratch each time, including for the coconut cold brew (95 THB).
- **Menu:** Descriptions are needed for the tourist half of customers.
- **Ordering:** Counter service at peak times leaves little room for AI; keep it manual.
- **Bookkeeping:** Daily records are repetitive but less urgent.

**Top picks (within 1,000 THB/month):**
1. **LINE reply drafts** for hours, menu, and order questions. This has the highest volume.
2. **Instagram caption drafts.** These are frequent and low-risk.
3. **English menu descriptions.** This is one-off work that is reused daily.

**Time savings:** Staff should log actual minutes on these tasks for one week before estimating savings. **Tool pricing:** Confirm costs before buying to stay under budget.

**Trial setup (Week 1–4)**

- **Tool:** Choose one AI chat tool with a free tier. Confirm its current limits (message caps, features) before using it.
- **Tasks:** LINE reply drafts for hours, menu, and order questions, plus Instagram caption drafts.
- **Prompts:** Write one reusable prompt per task. Each must state: "Bean There Cafe is open 08:00–17:00 only. Never mention or imply any other hours."
- **Hours check:** Staff check every LINE draft for times and open/closed statements before sending. Any draft with a time outside 08:00–17:00 is rejected and logged.
- **Weekly review:** Each Friday, staff score a sample of 10 outputs for accuracy, tone, and hours compliance, and record the results.
- **Budget check:** Track monthly usage against free-tier limits. If a limit is reached, pause the trial, or upgrade only if the total stays within 1,000 THB/month.
